⚠️ Spoiler boundary: Notes currently cover Volumes 1–14 only. Anything from Volume 15 onward should be considered spoiler territory.

👤 Main Characters

Rudeus Greyrat

Protagonist. Reincarnated into the Six-Faced World. Uses the name Rudeus.

Sylphiette (Sylphy)

Rudeus's childhood friend. Green-haired, half-human/half-Superd. Rudeus knew her as Fitz later on.

Eris Boreas Greyrat

Rudeus's former student/partner during the Demon Continent journey. Extremely strong swordswoman.

Roxy Migurdia

Rudeus's first major magic teacher. Migurd mage. Rudeus deeply respects her.

Paul Greyrat

Rudeus's father. Former adventurer and swordsman.

Zenith Greyrat

Rudeus's mother. Former adventurer and healer.

Lilia

Paul and Zenith's former maid. Also Paul's second wife.

Aisha Greyrat

Paul's daughter with Lilia; Rudeus's younger half-sister.

Norn Greyrat

Paul's daughter with Zenith; Rudeus's younger sister.

Ghislaine Dedoldia

Beastwoman swordswoman. Eris's bodyguard and Rudeus's sword instructor.

Ruijerd Superdia

Superd warrior who travels with Rudeus and Eris. Becomes extremely important to them.

Orsted — The Dragon God

Rudeus encounters him during the journey. Very important person to remember.

Nanahoshi Shizuka

Mysterious Japanese girl transported to the Six-Faced World. Connected to Rudeus's previous world.

Hitogami

The mysterious Human God who appears in Rudeus's dreams and gives him advice. Don't trust him blindly.

❤️ Relationships

Rudeus → Sylphy

Childhood friends → separated → reunited as Fitz. By the end of Vol. 12, they're married.

Rudeus → Roxy

Teacher → respected companion → romantic relationship develops. Roxy becomes one of Rudeus's wives.

Rudeus → Eris

Teacher/student → adventuring partners → lovers. Eris leaves to train after their night together because of her misunderstanding.

Rudeus → Paul

Father/son relationship. Complicated at times, but they ultimately care deeply about each other.

Rudeus → Zenith

Mother/son. Zenith's condition after the Begaritt Labyrinth is extremely important.

Rudeus → Norn & Aisha

Younger sisters. Norn has a complicated relationship with Rudeus because of her feelings about him. Aisha is extremely capable and generally very comfortable around Rudeus.

Rudeus → Ruijerd

Started as companions traveling together. Rudeus comes to deeply respect and trust him. Ruijerd becomes almost like a protective older-brother figure.

Rudeus → Orsted

Initially terrified because of Orsted's reputation/presence. Their encounter at the Millis Continent is one of the biggest turning points in Rudeus's life.
