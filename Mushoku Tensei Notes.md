### **Main Characters**

**\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_**



**Rudeus Greyrat:**

Protagonist. Reincarnated into the Six-Faced World. Uses the name Rudeus.



**Sylphiette (Sylphy):**

Rudeus's childhood friend. Green-haired, half-human/half-Superd. Rudeus knew her as Fitz later on.



**Eris Boreas Greyrat:**

Rudeus's former student/partner during the Demon Continent journey. Extremely strong swordswoman.



**Roxy Migurdia:**

Rudeus's first major magic teacher. Migurd mage. Rudeus deeply respects her.



**Paul Greyrat:**

Rudeus's father. Former adventurer and swordsman.



**Zenith Greyrat:**

Rudeus's mother. Former adventurer/healer.



**Lilia:**

Paul and Zenith's former maid. Also Paul's second wife.



**Aisha Greyrat:**

Paul's daughter with Lilia; Rudeus's younger half-sister.



**Norn Greyrat:**

Paul's daughter with Zenith; Rudeus's younger sister.



**Ghislaine Dedoldia:**

Beastwoman swordswoman. Eris's bodyguard and Rudeus's sword instructor.



**Ruijerd Superdia:**

Superd warrior who travels with Rudeus and Eris. Becomes extremely important to them.



**Orsted	The Dragon God:**

Rudeus encounters him during the journey. Very important person to remember.



**Nanahoshi Shizuka:**

Mysterious Japanese girl transported to the Six-Faced World. Connected to Rudeus's previous world.



**Hitogami:**

The mysterious Human God who appears in Rudeus's dreams and gives him advice. Don't trust him blindly.

### **Relationships**

**\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_**



**Rudeus → Sylphy**



&#x20;   Childhood friends → separated → reunited as Fitz.

&#x20;   By the end of Vol. 12, they're married.



**Rudeus → Roxy**



&#x20;   Teacher → respected companion → romantic relationship develops.

&#x20;   Roxy becomes one of Rudeus's wives.



**Rudeus → Eris**



&#x20;   Teacher/student → adventuring partners → lovers.

&#x20;   Eris leaves to train after their night together because of her misunderstanding.



**Rudeus → Paul**



&#x20;   Father/son relationship.

&#x20;   Complicated at times, but they ultimately care deeply about each other.



**Rudeus → Zenith**



&#x20;   Mother/son.

&#x20;   Zenith's condition after the Begaritt Labyrinth is extremely important.



**Rudeus → Norn \& Aisha**



&#x20;   Younger sisters.

&#x20;   Norn has a complicated relationship with Rudeus because of her feelings about him.

&#x20;   Aisha is extremely capable and generally very comfortable around Rudeus.



**Rudeus → Ruijerd**



&#x20;   Started as companions traveling together.

&#x20;   Rudeus comes to deeply respect and trust him.

&#x20;   Ruijerd becomes almost like a protective older-brother figure.



**Rudeus → Orsted**



&#x20;   Initially terrified because of Orsted's reputation/presence.

&#x20;   Their encounter at the Millis Continent is one of the biggest turning points in Rudeus's life.
